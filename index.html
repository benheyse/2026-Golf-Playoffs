Playoff Format
Full Official Rules ↗
Event 1: St. Jude Championship (18 players compete)
The 6 players with the lowest combined total (regular season + St. Jude) are eliminated.
Event 2: BMW Championship (12 players compete)
The 5 players with the lowest combined total (regular season + St. Jude + BMW) are eliminated.
Event 3: Tour Championship (7 players compete)
The final 7 compete under unique scoring...
Rank is determined by Total League Points (regular season + St. Jude + BMW) + the player's raw DraftKings score from the Tour Championship contest.
eg: Heysenberg has 590 Total League Points and scores 498 DK points in the Tour Champ for a grand total of 1,088 points.

Tiebreaker: Tied players share the same rank. In ties that determine advancement or prize money, both players advance and/or split prize money.

Scoring — Events 1 & 2

Events 1 & 2 award doubled league points. The Winner Bonus still applies and is doubled from 3pts to 6pts.

Note: No Cut Bonus does not apply. Playoff events are all No-Cut.
